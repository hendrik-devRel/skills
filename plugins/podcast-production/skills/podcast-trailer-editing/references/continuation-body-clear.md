# Continue an accepted kinetic pilot

Use this workflow when the user accepts a pilot and authorizes the remainder. Preserve the accepted interval and existing edit within the requested scope. Apply new placement constraints, such as keeping text off both faces and bodies, to the continuation. Fonts, colors, accent choices and shot rectangles below are examples from one trailer, not defaults for other footage.

## Establish the current clock

Snapshot the actual full sequence and camera cuts, then export current picture and native PCM from a work copy. Probe the exports: canvas, rational frame rate, decoded frames, audio samples and intended end. A successful export call does not establish that its range is correct. A music tail can extend the sequence beyond the last picture; resolve the intended range on the work copy, export again and verify the result.

Transcribe that current PCM, keeping source path/hash, zero origin, raw word times, chosen frame onsets and uncertainty. Use a second pass or focused crops for reduced words, repetitions and soft sentence endings. An older trailer script may have different removals and holds; do not derive current word times by shifting its clock by a guessed constant.

If a corrected export changes only the tail or container, compare decoded PCM over the entire region used by the timing map. Byte-identical PCM over that region supports reusing its times; record both file hashes and the exact comparison range. Similar duration alone does not. Preserve the original ASR and label any later selected-word corrections.

## Design for bodies and camera cuts

Inspect the current picture throughout each shot, including gestures, leaning, hands, inserts and transitions. Derive a conservative clear rectangle for each shot and keep glyphs, stroke, plates and underline extents inside it. A rectangle based on one convenient still may be invalid later in the same shot. Start with contact sheets, inspect full-size boundary and motion samples, then verify the final composite.

Store shot ranges as half-open integer frame intervals. Keep phrase membership, source word IDs, original onset frames and proposed line layouts separately from native operation batches. On a camera change within a phrase:

- End the old layout exactly at the cut.
- Copy already visible words into the new shot's fixed layout with `new_onset = max(original_onset, cut_frame)`.
- Retain future word onsets; omit words whose onset is at or after that layout's end.
- Preserve every selected source word even when it has multiple layer instances.

A word immediately before a cut can create a distracting one- or two-frame flash. A small, explicitly recorded delay to the incoming shot may be preferable; review it against speech rather than silently treating the new time as a transcription result. If review identifies an unsupported early word on the outgoing camera, disable that layout's copy and preserve its correctly timed incoming-shot occurrence; do not remove every layer containing the same word. Do not move previously revealed words around as later words arrive within a shot.

Use shorter line breaks and smaller type where a wide shot offers less space. In the worked continuation, host and guest shots had large side rectangles while the wide shot used a shallow area above both speakers. Approximately 40px of initial inset left room for accents and a 3px dark stroke. These margins and sizes must be derived again for different framing.

## Measure, lay out and accent natively

Use the [compiler contract](helper-contracts.md) for prepare → actual AE measurements → normalize → layout. A PIL/browser estimate is only preflight. Check the resolved native font family/style and variable axes. Keep the measurement signature and exact plan together; changing text/font/size before layout requires fresh measurements.

Audit actual baseline positions and advances against each shot rectangle, adding the declared vertical envelope and stroke allowance. Advances are not raster ink bounds. A passing numeric audit still needs native rendered-frame review.

Add selective native accents only after the measured text positions are fixed. Use native `layer.bounds`, transformed into composition coordinates, to size plates around actual glyph ink, descenders and stroke. Advances and cap-height envelopes are useful preflight estimates; replace them before final rendering. Add explicit padding that stays inside the picture-derived clear rectangle and check compact stacked-plate gaps visually. Derive underline spans from the same ink union. A left-anchored rectangle can grow from Scale `[0,100]` to `[100,100]` over five frames, with its first key exactly at the layer in-point to prevent backward extrapolation.

Check actual glyph/background overlap in the composite at the intended output viewing size, especially over lamps and other bright objects. Large white words over a white lamp do not pass readability review solely because a thin stroke or numeric bounds pass. When orange plates are part of the established treatment, add a matching plate selectively behind affected words. Size it from visible ink, including Q descenders and stroke, rather than cap height alone. Preserve word onsets and out-points, keep adjacent small words clear of the plate, and recheck the phrase through its reveals and background motion.

Use semantic shape names and bounded serial batches. If the comp initially contains only N text layers, create each new shape and move it to the current bottom index N+1, N+2, and so on. Confirm the actual count first; do not use this arithmetic after adding a guide or unrelated layer. A contrast pass can set only stroke properties on nonplate words without changing their font, position or timing. Read all native results and warnings before continuing.

Render useful intermediate and completed layouts. Check wide-shot type, text over bright backgrounds, accent spacing and camera-cut copies. Test editability in a temporary duplicate, verify the changed word in a render, then remove only that test duplicate and save the working AEP.

## Add brand marks selectively

Use supplied or verified brand artwork as imported footage, preserving its pixels and aspect ratio. Record its source page and file hash. Measure a blank area beside the relevant model name inside the same body-clear rectangle; include any separate contrast tile in the bounds audit. Use comparable visual weight for competing companies. A short-lived model word may need the logo to appear with the preceding phrase so the mark is readable. Omit repeated logo flashes when the adjacent longer mention communicates the same association. Retain existing publisher branding on the end card when that already serves the edit.

Align an inline logotype using its visible **letter** baseline, not the bottom or center of a taller circular symbol. Measure the wordmark's own ink bounds inside the asset, transform them at the intended uniform scale, and compare with adjacent text. When enlarging a standalone logo cue, preserve the chosen optical center and recheck clear space, timing and entrance motion.

An official SVG or transparent PNG may still paint the mark's internal negative space an opaque dark color. Inspect path fills and rendered alpha at an interior point; a transparent outer background is insufficient. If the user explicitly requests a knockout and no suitable official asset exists, preserve the untouched download and make a clearly labelled derived variant using the identical supplied path as an alpha mask. Keep contours, proportions and lettering unchanged. Rasterize through a standard SVG renderer when required by the editing app, then verify interior alpha, white artwork, unchanged wordmark geometry and the composite over a colored background. Record source URLs, hashes and the derivation; do not present the variant as an unchanged official download.

A separate, silent brand-overlay composition can keep the completed typography render unchanged and provide independent timing and placement in Premiere. Render it using the same source-time contract and run the same every-frame bounds check. In the worked example, two 160px UHD tiles sat beside the guest's model mentions; the accepted first ten seconds and CodeRabbit end card stayed unchanged.

## Refine an approved trailer without retiming

When the user likes the existing timing and asks for visual polish, duplicate the current review/graphics and any end-card composition before changing them. Preserve every word's source identity, in/out points, camera cuts, grades and established audio repairs. If the user clarifies that a call-to-action should be removed, disable its specific native text layer in the duplicated title rather than moving it or removing the surrounding end card.

For small-screen readability, reflow dense rows before scaling the entire overlay. Give selected emphasis words their own rows and enlarge connecting text where the measured clear rectangle allows it. Inspect effective size: a nominal 64px font at 75% transform scale renders at 48px. If replacing that arrangement with a new explicit font size, reset the old scale deliberately to avoid applying the reduction twice. A long word already filling a narrow body-clear column may need to retain its width while the surrounding type grows. Keep glyph proportions uniform; do not widen the column into a moving head to satisfy a blanket size increase. Review both full-size and small-screen composites.

When a logo should replace each spoken brand mention, inventory the exact word layers and use the official full artwork at those same reveal and clear frames. Preserve its aspect ratio, suppress the replaced text layer, and measure the logo together with adjacent words and accent padding. A wordmark-plus-symbol needs different optical alignment and space than ordinary text. This replacement behavior follows the user's brief; it does not change the selective-logo guidance for unrelated projects.

Recompute accents from fresh native glyph bounds after resizing and reflowing. For a plate attached to one moving word, parent it to that word while preserving its composition-space placement **before** adding motion; verify that parenting has not shifted or scaled the plate twice. Retain stroke/descender padding and inspect the entrance as well as the settled frame. A restrained worked option at 1080p is a four-frame, 4px upward settle with uniform 98%→100% scale and ease-out, no overshoot. Start at the existing word in-point with visible opacity; shorten the settle for a shorter word hold rather than delaying its onset. Keep established underline reveals unless the brief changes them. These values are an option, not a style requirement.

For a remaining low vocal reaction, use the available [audio-restoration skill](../../audio-restoration/SKILL.md) and its shared-mix backchannel workflow. Inspect each gap in context; a voiced acknowledgment is not stationary electrical hum. Apply an additional reduction only to individually reviewed intervals and record its cumulative effect when an earlier dip already exists. Preserve decoded dialogue outside the selected intervals, the music/mix residual, the exact sample count and previously repaired word endings. A before/after ASR match supports content preservation but does not establish natural sound or speaker identity. Keep matched original/reduced/removed previews and disclose when no subjective audition occurred.

Separately adjustable speaking-turn files from a shared recording remain shared-mix selections, not recovered microphone ISOs. Enable either their verified reconstruction plus music/residual or the complete replacement mix, never both. An arithmetic original-mix-minus-dialogue file should retain its residual label unless its contents have been established as music alone. After muxing the final picture with current PCM, repeat the audio alignment and complete-decode checks in [verification and delivery](verification-delivery.md); a good typography preview does not validate the final soundtrack.

## Preserve source time when placing the alpha

Record both the rendered asset's source origin and its intended sequence origin. For sequence frames `[S,E)` there are two valid arrangements:

|Alpha asset|Premiere source in/out|Premiere placement|
|---|---|---|
|Rendered only from AE frames S through E−1; asset frame 0 represents sequence frame S|`[0,E−S)`|Timeline start S, end E|
|Rendered from AE frame 0, retaining the full composition clock|`[S,E)`|Timeline start S, end E|

Inserting source frame 0 of a full-duration alpha at timeline S would delay its existing animation by S. Verify native clip source in/out, timeline start/end and representative entrance frames after insertion. Keep microphones, music, camera lines and existing accepted graphics intact; the alpha should have no audio stream. Check duplicate captions and old titles from the actual native sequence.

For the historical continuation example, S=600 and E=2771 at 60000/1001, so a bounded alpha contains 2171 frames. Text clears before two black transition frames; the endcard begins at 2773. These numbers describe that edit only.

## Check every alpha frame and the composite

[`scripts/check_alpha_bounds.py`](../scripts/check_alpha_bounds.py) is a read-only checker for silent ProRes4444-family overlays. It takes an explicit JSON shot/zone contract and a new report path. It decodes every frame, checks source dimensions/rate, silence, expected frame count, nonempty artwork somewhere, and alpha occupancy within the scheduled clear rectangle. Frames in schedule gaps must be transparent. Individual blank frames are counted and allowed; an entirely blank movie fails.

```sh
python3 /path/to/podcast-trailer-editing/scripts/check_alpha_bounds.py \
  --input /path/to/continuation-alpha.mov \
  --config /path/to/current-alpha-config.json \
  --report /path/to/new-alpha-report.json
```

The [historical example config](../assets/examples/continuation-alpha-config.json) demonstrates the contract; derive a new one from the current source. Fields are `schema_version:1`, rational `fps`, `[width,height]` `size` and `decode_size`, `global_start_frame`, `global_end_frame_exclusive`, named `zones` as `[left,top,right,bottom]` pixel edges, and ordered nonoverlapping `shots` with integer `start_frame`, `end_frame_exclusive` and `zone`. Right/bottom edges are exclusive. A full-duration asset can use global start 0 with transparent schedule gaps; a bounded asset uses its actual source-frame origin S.

`decode_size` must be an equal integer reduction of both dimensions, from 1× through 4×. The checker binarizes any nonzero alpha before full-range area reduction so faint single-pixel edges survive. Bounds are quantized at that scale; `tolerance_pixels` defaults to the reduction factor and cannot exceed it. Use 1×/zero tolerance when exact source-pixel bounds are necessary. Reports disclose the resolution, tolerance, source-frame mapping and every frame's bounds. Required runtime: Python with NumPy, FFmpeg and ffprobe. Existing reports are never overwritten. Exit 0 means pass, 1 means a completed failing check, 2 means an input/configuration error. `--self-test` checks masks, cut mapping and actual faint-alpha extraction through FFmpeg without creating media files.

The generic checker was independently exercised on an isolated six-frame ProRes4444 fixture: a valid three-occupied/three-blank-frame render passed; out-of-zone alpha at a camera cut, alpha in a transparent gap, a wrong source origin and a frame-count mismatch each failed as expected. This validates those checker behaviors, not a trailer render.

This proves containment in the supplied rectangles, not automatic person detection or speech sync. The source-frame origin is a caller-supplied contract; it cannot be inferred from rendered pixels. Decode the complete final composite as well. Inspect representative completed/intermediate phrases, every camera-cut transition and the last-text/black/endcard boundary; view the full duration for motion and timing. Distinguish sampled still review from full playback. Verify preview audio against the authoritative current PCM using [preview_audio.py](helper-contracts.md#audio-offset-verification-and-replacement); correct only measured export delay and recheck the result. A good external review render does not establish native Premiere integration.
