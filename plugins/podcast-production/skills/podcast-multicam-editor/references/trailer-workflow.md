# Source-grounded podcast trailers

Use when the user requests a trailer, teaser, cold open, or shortlist of compelling passages. The episode edit remains the first priority unless the user changes it.

## Editorial selection

- Read the actual timestamped interview and relevant company/editorial brief. Company relevance is a selection lens, not permission to put product claims in the guest's mouth.
- For CodeRabbit work, connect naturally to the current content theaters and company narrative: AI-generated changes, review and evidence, human judgment, agentic change management, security or open-source collaboration. Re-read the supplied/current brief rather than freezing a launch message in this skill.
- Prefer a concrete surprise or tension, a short explanation, and an unresolved question or payoff. Around 55–65 seconds is a useful interpretation of “about a minute”; do not fill time with repeated branding.
- Keep exact words, original source in/out, handles, speaker, context, and the reason for selection. Separate spoken claims from proposed editorial captions. Avoid isolated negations, missing antecedents, hypothetical statements presented as incidents, or synthetic endorsements.
- Separate episode footage from external conference talks, promotional clips, or interviews. Use other sources only when authorized and identified.

## Assembly

Map transcript timestamps to the verified master clock. Refine cut boundaries by listening to source audio and retain natural breaths. Reordering may sharpen the story but must not imply that statements were consecutive or change what they mean.

Create a separate native Premiere project when requested; an additional sequence inside the episode project alone does not meet that request. Keep the clean episode and tightened episode unmodified. Do not prepend the trailer until the user approves that assembly.

Use guest close-ups for key statements and deliberate wide/reaction shots at joins. Avoid misleading reaction shots. Music, title cards, graphics, sound effects and third-party footage are optional, not assumed. Verify ownership/licensing and scope before using them; a file in an Assets folder is not necessarily the correct guest name or final approved asset.

## Handoff

Deliver the editable trailer plus a source-time selects list with alternate hooks and context risks. Check duration, intelligibility, each source range and the first/last syllables. Clearly label a rough assembly as a draft; a transcript-only shortlist is not a finished trailer.
